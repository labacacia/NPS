## Decision

Accept the first complete fair measurement and retain its negative resource outcome.
The first matrix alone did not close D20-05. The controlled qualification below supplies the missing resource evidence.
All80conditions have outcomes;78succeed,2remain failed. This acceptance is not a claim
that every model answer is correct or that the whole suite/adopter envelope is qualified.
The runtime must continue rejecting fabricated/stale/cross-owner/uninspected references;
never repair model text into a reported success. Authority and descriptor ownership stay
in the runtime. Future model-facing short evidence handles may avoid copying long
identifiers, but require separate evidence and cannot relabel these failed cases.

Keep data-results/invocation and schema-batch profiles experimental, explicitly
selected and default-off. Native result binding and conditional derived/stream contracts
remain unsupported/unregistered. No additional result action or frame number is added.
Keep the ordinary HTTP baseline equivalent and capable of external computation.

Continue authorized D20-06 and D20-07 after the controlled resource AC recorded below. Evaluate practical
value in authorized result handoff, bounded retry/cancellation certainty, discovery
freshness and explicit process-state ownership. References pass through trusted runtime
mappings and current NIP/resource policy; model text does not create authority or a
verified execution outcome. Their own AC and phase gates remain mandatory.

This decision is the required equal/worse-result scope/design review before broad
porting. Broad implementations may pursue interoperable contracts and measured workflow
semantics; they cannot inherit an efficiency or reliability claim from this demo.
D20-08 must qualify every claimed SDK/binding and retain unsupported cells explicitly.
